# Casa Ta Verde — design direction

## 2. Design thesis: the inhabited section
A calm architectural showroom moves from a desirable home to an understandable section through its systems. A panoramic forest residence establishes the destination; precise numbering, a custom section drawing and connected editorial rows explain the decisions behind it. Visitors can enter through one product or plan several systems without being forced into a package.

- Warm stone canvas, forest ink and a single restrained sage accent.
- One geometric family; generous but readable headlines and small technical captions.
- Landscape crops and open rows, not boxed feature-card grids.
- Technical annotations earn their place by explaining a system relationship.
- Every major section offers a relevant path toward a useful project brief.

Composition: broad margins and a left-aligned text spine; split headline/description above a wide architectural panorama. A horizontal family index bridges into the interactive section. Alternate open pale sections, a forest audience panel and a final quiet call to action.

Motion: framed architectural photographs travel with native scrolling; section groups settle into place and the house systems assemble once on entry. Selected systems retain their local response. Text stays fully opaque throughout. See website-motion-casa-ta-verde.md for device policies and timings.

Distinctiveness: section-drawing vocabulary, six consistent numbered system markers, a panoramic gabled forest house and the relationship between warm architectural material and technical precision.

Rejected: floating glass dashboard, green gradients, glowing connections, fake statistics, endless three-card grids, oversized empty hero, scroll-jacking.

Weakest point: an aspirational image can be mistaken for a delivered project. Keep the concept label directly adjacent and do not present a fabricated portfolio.

## 4. Visual system
- Typeface: self-hosted Manrope variable, Latin + Latin Extended for Romanian, SIL OFL retained. Display 40–76px, section 32–52px, body 16–18px, utility 12–14px. Line heights 1.08 display / 1.65 body. Long text measure ~65 characters.
- Tokens: canvas #f6f5f0, paper #ffffff, ink #223d31, muted #5e685e, forest #244b38, pale sage #e7ebe1, hairline #d7dbd2. Off-white text on forest; dark text on pale backgrounds. Verify contrast in rendered audit.
- Spacing: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96/128px. Content max 1440px, fluid 20–64px side margins. Desktop 12-column conceptual grid; two-column editorial splits; mobile single column.
- Shape: nearly square imagery and panels, gently rounded 4px buttons; rounded number markers only. Hairlines for editorial separation; no card shadows.
- Imagery: generated architecture clearly marked concept, natural woodland palette, no text baked into image. Responsive AVIF + WebP, dimensions, early hero and lazy secondary. Original SVG drawing with HTML explanations; never represented as construction documentation.
- Interactions: forest primary button / underlined tertiary links; strong external focus outline, 44px minimum key controls. Selected systems have marker + background + text state, not color alone.
- Forms: empty fields use persistent labels; validation gives inline guidance and focus to invalid input; pending disables submission only; confirmed success announces delivery; unavailable/error retains fields and exposes copy/download fallback. Copy failure has a download alternative.
- Mobile: ~40px headline, 20px margins, 300–360px-high architectural crop, stacked CTA links and sections. Tablet intermediate columns. TV content stays bounded for readable measure.
- Identity survives removal of shadows/gradients: none are required.
